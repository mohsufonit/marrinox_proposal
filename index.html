02  ·  Problems

Three areas to improve on the live Mariinox site

Found by going through mariinox.com, the About Us and Team pages, the campaign pages at /lp and /lp-6 and the Meta Ad Library. About 78 active Meta ads send paid traffic to /lp and /lp-6, so the homepage is where branded searchers, investors and partners arrive.

PROBLEM 01  ·  CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE
Solved by the customer homepage →

The homepage carries a lot of text, and the estimate form sits below the first screen

The homepage carries about 2,100 words of visible text across nine sections. Sixteen more FAQ answers sit behind clicks. Many sections explain in paragraphs what a picture or a number could show at a glance.

HIGHEST PRIORITY  ·  FIRST SCREEN

On a phone, the first form field starts below the first screen

The hero stacks a pill, a three line headline, a support line, three promise chips, two proof stats and four photos before the estimate form. At 390px the first field sits 872px down on an 844px screen, and the sticky Get an estimate bar covers the form heading.

HIGHEST PRIORITY  ·  TEXT DENSITY

The impact section carries the most text on the page

It holds about 365 words, the most of any section. The certificates section carries about 230. Both could lead with a number or a visual and open to the detail.

MID PRIORITY  ·  REPETITION

The process appears twice

The page covers five traceability checkpoints and later five steps of how scrapping works. Both follow the same path from handover to certificate, so one visual can carry both.

PROBLEM 02  ·  INVESTOR READINESS
Solved by the investor layer →

The page can do more for investors, and a few numbers need to line up

The word investor does not appear on the homepage, the About Us page or the Team page. The business case is on the site in pieces, in different places, and a few of the pieces differ from each other.

HIGHEST PRIORITY  ·  CREDIBILITY

The scale figures on the homepage differ

The hero says 10,000+ vehicles scrapped. The impact band says 1,923 families served. The steel counter, 846 T, equals 1,923 cars at the page's own 440 kg each. The carbon counter, 6,420 T, equals about 9,900 cars at its own 0.65 t each. The page's code also marks these counters as placeholders awaiting live facility data.

HIGHEST PRIORITY  ·  POSITIONING

The company is described in three slightly different ways

The hero says Mariinox operates a network of facilities authorised by the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways. The footer says Mariinox is not a government agency and has no affiliation with the Ministry beyond its facilities' authorisations. Google's description of the About Us page calls it a govt-certified car scrapping facility. The page speaks of a network but names one facility, Sega Tech Solution Pvt Ltd. An investor has to piece the operating model together from the FAQ and the footer.

MID PRIORITY  ·  MISSING PROOF

The strongest investor facts are easy to miss

The NITI Aayog projection of close to 50 million end of life vehicles by 2030 sits inside a paragraph in the impact section. A capacity of 6,000+ vehicles a month appears only on the About Us page. No OEM, fleet customer or press name appears on either page, though the About Us page calls Mariinox a trusted partner of government agencies and automotive manufacturers.

PROBLEM 03  ·  ABOUT US PAGE
Solved by the About Us page →

About Us can tell more of the company story

The page runs about 1,070 words and covers mission, vision, values and a facility gallery. It carries no traction number, and the three co-founders sit on a separate Team page.

HIGHEST PRIORITY  ·  PROOF

Certifications are listed, not shown

ISO 14001:2015, ISO 9001:2015 and the RVSF licence appear as headings and sentences. No certificate, number or issuing body is shown.

MID PRIORITY  ·  TRACTION

The numbers shown are facility specs, not results

10,000+ sq ft, 6,000+ vehicles a month of capacity and a 90% recovery target are the only figures. Vehicles scrapped, cities served and customer ratings do not appear.

MID PRIORITY  ·  COPY

Two values carry the same sentence

Integrity and Compliance both read, We operate with complete transparency and honesty in all our dealings. Compliance is the value a scrapping business stands on, and it has no line of its own.
